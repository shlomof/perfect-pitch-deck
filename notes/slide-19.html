Beat: Foundation next to the Full Package, so she sees what the extra money buys.

Say: "Two ways to start. Foundation is the Kit build with your pledge sign-up page, and the welcome email. You have a home for the list and you run it yourself. Watch out: Do not push Foundation. Read the table once, then ask which fits and stop talking. Foundation has no question flow, so it does not deliver the who-is-who promise from Slide 5: say that plainly. Not in either column: visible pledge features, and inviting her LinkedIn connections to join (Dripify, her account, billed to her; only first-degree connections; she sends the follow-up herself). Price both once you know her connection count. The 3-month minimum is Full Package only.
