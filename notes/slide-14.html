Unique Mechanism — Slide 14

Beat: Name the mechanism plainly, tie it back to his own Call 1 objection.

Say: "This isn't cold outreach dressed up. The system does the hard part automatically — it tells you who's actually ready. Like I said on our first call: nobody buys a £19,500 program off the internet, you still have to make the calls. This just makes sure you're making them to the right people."

Watch out: Avoid the word 'unique' — let the mechanism itself do the differentiating. Don't frame the manual-call step as a deliberate feature to be grateful for ("so no bot tries to close your program") — that reads as excusing a limitation. Lead with what's automated (the finding), then state the human close as an unremarkable fact about how £19,500 sales work, not a selling point in itself.
