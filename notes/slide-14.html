Slide 14: Why Asset Bolt

Beat: Differentiate from a "here's your login" consultant. You build and run it, he reviews and approves.

Say: "Most consultants hand you a login and call it done. I build the whole system and run it, you review and approve, I handle the scraping and DM automation, backed by a technical team."

Watch out: Don't gloss over "you review and approve," that line is the plain-language version of the one-minute daily check-in, say it clearly.
