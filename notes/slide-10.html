Objections, Slide 10

Beat: Handle three doubts before she raises them: marketing, answering questions, her time.

Say: "You might be thinking this is just more marketing. It is not, it is data on who is ready to talk to you. You might wonder if people will answer questions: it is one or two, answered with a click when they sign up. And your time: you write the content, I build it, and I need a couple of approvals a week."

Watch out: The old list is not on this slide on purpose. She never raised it as a worry. It is your question, not her objection: who owns it, can she mail it, when was the last send. If she cannot mail it, say "Then we build from zero, and that is what the pledge and the LinkedIn group are for."
