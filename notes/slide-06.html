Key Outcomes, Slide 6

Beat: Three outcomes: know who is who, own the audience, calls with context.

Say: "First, you know who is a hybrid professional, who leads a company stuck on return-to-office, and who books speakers. Second, your followers and your old list become one list that is yours. Third, a speaking or consulting call starts with what the person told you."

Watch out: Ask before the price: what one consulting engagement or speaking booking is worth to her, who is paying, and how many people she expects to join in the first six weeks and from where. The offer sorts and captures people, it does not bring them. You told her on Call 1 that you do not do the outreach. Her sources: the LinkedIn group (about five join requests a day, ask who owns it), her curated connections, the pledge, and the presale campaign.
