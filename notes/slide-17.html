Today's Offer — Slide 17

Beat: Name the offer and the price together, confidently, no hedging.

Say: "Here's the offer: the Full Pipeline System, $6,500 to build it, $1,700 a month to keep it running after that, live in five to six weeks. Let me show you what's actually in the $6,500."

Watch out: Don't over-explain the price here — that's the guarantee slide's job. Say the retainer number once, clearly, and move on.
