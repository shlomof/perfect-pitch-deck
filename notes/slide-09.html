The First Six Weeks, Slide 9

Beat: Week by week, from kickoff to the first read of the data. Syncs land on weeks 2, 4 and 6.

Say: "It takes about six weeks. Week one is the kickoff call and the accounts. Two and three, your sign-up page, welcome email and the questions. Week four, the answers become tags and I test all of it. Week five it goes live, and people start joining. Week six we read the first data together."

Watch out: This is the plan for the build, not a promise of results. If the 15,000 list can be mailed, the re-invite is planned after go-live; do not put a date on it until you know who owns it. Her approvals can move a week.
